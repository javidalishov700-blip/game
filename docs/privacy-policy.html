SLICE BLAST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Slice & Blast does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs in the app.

The short version. The game runs entirely on your device. Nothing you do in it leaves your phone, and there is no server for it to leave to.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, process or transmit any of the following:

  • Name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • Account credentials — the game has no sign-in of any kind
  • Location, precise or approximate
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA), device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry or performance traces
  • Purchase history — the game contains no purchases of any kind

The app requests no runtime permissions and includes no advertising, analytics or attribution frameworks.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves three small settings locally, using Apple's standard app preferences storage:

  • Your best score
  • Whether sound is on or off
  • Whether vibration is on or off

This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us or by anyone else, and is deleted when you delete the app. If your device has iCloud backup enabled, these preferences may be included in your own encrypted device backup, which is under your control and governed by Apple's privacy policy rather than ours.

3. Network access

The game makes no network requests during play. The only time it can open a connection is when you deliberately tap the “Privacy Policy” or “Terms of Use” link on the title screen, which hands the address to your browser. Your browser, not the game, then makes that request.

4. Children

Slice & Blast is suitable for all ages and is designed so that it can be: because it collects nothing at all, there is nothing to collect from a child either. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Third parties

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose personal data, because we hold none. The app embeds no third-party advertising, analytics, social or attribution services.

Apple distributes the app and may collect its own information — for example download and aggregate performance statistics — under Apple's Privacy Policy. That processing is Apple's, not ours, and we receive no personal data from it.

6. Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and erase the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase. To remove the locally stored settings described in section 2, delete the app from your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of the page will change with it. Continued use of the app after an update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
