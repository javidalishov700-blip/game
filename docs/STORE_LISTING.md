# App Store listing — Slice & Blast 1.2

Copy each block into App Store Connect → Distribution → iOS App 1.2. English (U.S.) is the
primary language. Turkish and Russian are optional extra localizations (language menu at the
top right of the page → add Turkish / Russian); the game itself now speaks both.

Things fixed from the previous text, because App Review checks the description against the app:

- **Continue ad**: the rewarded continue gives *one more, smaller* block, not a bigger one.
- **Steel**: it moves at normal speed; "heavy, fast" was inaccurate.
- **"No data collected"** removed: AdMob collects data for ads, and the listing must match the
  App Privacy answers.
- Added what 1.2 actually has: Workshop, themes, daily missions, ranks, leaderboard, languages,
  resume.

### Description

```
Tap. Drop. Land it dead centre.

Slice & Blast is a one-thumb stacking game about precision. Every block swings across the top of your tower and waits for one tap. Land it perfectly and you keep your full width. Miss, and the overhang is sliced off and gone for good — and the tower gets that little bit harder.

Chain three perfect drops and the top of the tower detonates. Keep the streak alive and every blast gets bigger — five blocks, seven, nine — until the whole sky lights up.

RARE BLOCKS, REAL REWARDS
Every so often something else slides in, and each one behaves like what it looks like:

• NEON — land it and it charges the three layers beneath it, then takes them with it.
• ELECTRIC — arcs circling its shell. Land it and a current runs the height of the tower, doubling every point for fifteen seconds.
• GLASS — a shield that absorbs one mistake completely.
• STEEL — solid metal that widens the tower back out.

Miss one and it simply shatters. Specials never damage your tower — they are pure upside.

THE WORKSHOP
Blasts pay coins. Spend them on:
• Upgrades — Magnet (a wider perfect window), Armour (start every run shielded) and Fortune (specials arrive sooner)
• Nine themes that repaint the sky, the stars and every block
• Daily missions with coin rewards and a day streak
• Your builder rank, from Rookie to Legend, and your lifetime stats

BUILT TO BE PICKED UP
• One tap, no tutorial, no menus in the way
• Leave mid-run and pick up exactly where you left off
• Game Center leaderboard — the top 99 all-time scores
• English, Turkish and Russian
• No purchases and no accounts
• Free with the occasional ad — a short one between runs, and an optional one that gives you one more block to continue a run
```
