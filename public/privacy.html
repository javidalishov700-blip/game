FLINCH

Privacy Policy — last updated 14 August 2026

FLINCH is a single-player timing game. It does not require an account and does not include ads, analytics, or third-party trackers in version 1.0.

Data we store on your device

Your best score is saved locally on the device (web: localStorage; native: the app sandbox). This value never leaves your device.

Data we do not collect

  • Name, email, or other identity
  • Location
  • Advertising ID / tracking
  • Gameplay uploaded to a server

Internet

The game runs fully offline after install. It does not send personal data over the network.

Children

FLINCH is a general-audience reflex game. We do not knowingly collect data from children because we collect no personal data at all.
